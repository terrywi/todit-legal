Todit Data Deletion

App: Todit · Package: com.todit.todaydoit · Developer: TerryWi · Effective 4 October 2026

This document is a translation of the Korean original. If the translation differs from the original, the Korean original prevails.

Todit is a personal to-do app for capturing tasks and managing them by date, repetition and reminders. As a rule, to-do records are stored only on the user’s device, and the Provider does not operate its own separate server that stores users’ to-do data. Users can delete their own data, or request deletion, in the following ways.

1. Delete in the app (processed immediately)

  • Delete a task — swipe the row left and choose “Delete”: the item is kept in “Recently deleted” for 30 days so it can be restored, then permanently deleted from the device.
  • Delete Drive backups — Settings > My info > Connected account > “Disconnect account & delete backups”: all backups stored in the app-specific folder (appDataFolder) of the user’s own Google Drive are deleted immediately via the Google Drive API, the Drive permission granted to the app is revoked, and the Google sign-in is disconnected.
  • Delete one Drive backup — Settings > My info > “Restore from Drive” > delete icon to the right of a backup: only the chosen backup file is deleted immediately via the Google Drive API.
  • Sign out — Settings > My info > “Sign out”: the Google sign-in is disconnected immediately. The Drive backup stays, so it can be restored after signing in again.
  • Delete records on this device — Settings > My info > “Reset data”: all tasks, subtasks and history stored on this device are deleted immediately. The Drive backup stays.
  • Uninstall the app: all local records stored on the device, including anything still in “Recently deleted”, are deleted along with it.

2. Request deletion by email

Because the Provider operates no server holding users’ to-do data, everything to be deleted is on the user’s device and in the user’s own Google Drive. If you would still like help with the deletion steps, or confirmation regarding deletion related to account linking, you can write to wwwtaekyu@gmail.com. The Provider will act without delay (within 7 business days) and reply with the outcome. Please put “Data deletion request” in the subject line.

3. What is deleted and what is retained

Data typeWhere it is storedHow / when it is deletedRetention after deletion
To-do records (tasks and subtasks, notes, dates and times, repetition and reminder settings, completion history, app settings) User’s device (local SQLite) Immediately on “Reset data” or on uninstalling the app None (not held on any server)
Deleted tasks User’s device (“Recently deleted”) Permanently deleted 30 days after deletion (immediately if the app is uninstalled) Up to 30 days
Google Drive backup files (JSON copy of local records) The user’s own Google Drive appDataFolder Immediately with the delete icon in the “Restore from Drive” list (one backup) or “Disconnect account & delete backups” (all) None (up to the 3 most recent backups are kept; backups are made only when the user taps “Back up now”, and the app never backs up automatically)
App data included in the Android device backup The Android device backup of the user’s Google account (Google) Turn it off in the device’s backup settings, or delete the device backup from the Google account Per the operating system (Google) policy; the operating system decides when backups and restores happen
Google sign-in information (email, account identifier) Google authentication (OAuth) Disconnected immediately on “Sign out” or “Disconnect account & delete backups” None
Audio spoken during voice input Not stored by Todit (processed by the device’s speech recognition service) — Subject to the speech recognition provider’s (usually Google’s) policies
Advertising identifier, device information, diagnostic data Google AdMob (Google LLC) Reset the Ad ID or turn off personalization in device Settings > Privacy > Ads Subject to Google’s privacy policy and retention periods

To keep your records before changing devices, you can export them as a JSON file under Settings > My info > “Export to file”. For details of how personal information is processed, please see the Todit Privacy Policy.
